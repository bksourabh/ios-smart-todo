Frequently Asked Questions

How does Smart Auto-Location work?

When you add a task with Smart mode (the default), the app automatically analyzes your task description and detects relevant location categories. For example, if you add "Buy medicines", Smart Todo will notify you when you're near ANY of your saved pharmacies. No manual location selection needed!

What categories does Smart Auto-Location support?

Smart Todo recognizes many categories including: Pharmacies (medicines, prescriptions), Fuel Stations (petrol, gas), Supermarkets (groceries, shopping), Restaurants, Banks, Post Offices, Hardware Stores, Electronics Stores, and Clothing Stores.

How do I enable location-based reminders?

Go to iOS Settings → Smart Todo → Location, and select "Always". This allows the app to notify you when you arrive at saved locations, even when the app is closed.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Make sure notifications are enabled in iOS Settings → Smart Todo → Notifications. Also check that Do Not Disturb mode is turned off.

How do I add a point of interest?

Tap the location pin icon in the top navigation bar. You can either use Suggestions to find nearby places, or tap the + button to manually add a location.

Is my data backed up?

Smart Todo stores all data locally on your device. Your data is included in your iCloud or iTunes backups if you have those enabled.

How do I delete my account?

Simply uninstall the app to remove all your data. You can also manage your Sign in with Apple credentials in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID.
