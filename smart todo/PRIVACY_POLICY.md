# Privacy Policy for Smart Todo

**Last Updated: February 7, 2026**

## Overview

Smart Todo ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID user identifier and optionally your name and email (if you choose to share them). This information is stored locally on your device.
- **Task Data**: All tasks, due dates, and notification preferences you create are stored locally on your device.
- **Location Data**: If you enable location-based reminders, we access your device's location to trigger notifications when you're near saved places. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to external servers.
- **Calendar & Reminders Data**: If you choose to import from your Calendar or Reminders, we access this data solely to create tasks within Smart Todo. Calendar and Reminders data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to external servers.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: We do not collect device information or analytics.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:
- Provide task management functionality
- Send you notification reminders at scheduled times
- Send you location-based reminders when you're near saved places
- Authenticate your identity via Sign in with Apple
- Import and analyze Calendar events and Reminders to create tasks (when you choose to use this feature)
- Analyze task content using on-device Apple Intelligence to detect categories and match locations (all processing happens locally on your device)

## Apple Intelligence

On supported devices (iPhone 15 Pro and later running iOS 26+), Smart Todo uses Apple Intelligence to:
- Understand and categorize your tasks
- Identify actionable items when importing from Calendar and Reminders
- Match tasks to relevant locations

All Apple Intelligence processing happens entirely on your device. Your task content is never sent to external servers for AI processing.

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: All your data (tasks, locations, preferences) is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not upload your data to any external servers.
- **Sign in with Apple**: Authentication is handled securely by Apple's systems.
- **On-Device Processing**: All AI analysis and location matching happens locally on your device.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data stays on your device.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Apple services:
- **Sign in with Apple**: For secure authentication
- **Apple Maps**: For location search and display
- **Local Notifications**: For task reminders
- **EventKit**: For reading Calendar events and Reminders (with your permission)
- **Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models**: For on-device task analysis (iOS 26+ on supported devices)

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all your data by uninstalling the app
- Revoke location permissions in iOS Settings
- Revoke Calendar and Reminders permissions in iOS Settings
- Manage Sign in with Apple credentials in your Apple ID settings

## Children's Privacy

Smart Todo does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
