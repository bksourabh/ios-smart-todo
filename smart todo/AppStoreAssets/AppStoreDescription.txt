APP NAME: Smart Todo

SUBTITLE: AI-Powered Task Reminders

DESCRIPTION:
Smart Todo is the intelligent task manager that automatically knows where you need to be. Just add a task like "Buy medicines" and Smart Todo figures out the rest - powered by Apple Intelligence on supported devices.

KEY FEATURES:

• Apple Intelligence Integration
On iPhone 15 Pro and later, Smart Todo uses Apple Intelligence to understand your tasks with remarkable accuracy. The on-device AI analyzes natural language to detect context and intent, even for complex or nuanced task descriptions. All processing happens privately on your device.

• Calendar & Reminders Import
Seamlessly import your existing iOS Calendar events and Reminders. Smart Todo intelligently analyzes each item using Apple Intelligence to identify actionable tasks - automatically filtering out questions and notes while keeping real to-dos like "Buy groceries" or "Pick up prescription". Select individual items or use bulk select/deselect for quick import. Imported tasks are automatically matched with your saved locations for smart notifications.

• Smart Auto-Location
The magic of AI meets task management. Simply add a task and Smart Todo automatically detects relevant locations. Add "Buy medicines" and get notified near ALL your saved pharmacies. Add "Get fuel" and get reminded at ANY nearby petrol station. No manual setup required.

• Intelligent Category Detection
Smart Todo understands your tasks:
  - Pharmacies: medicines, prescriptions, vitamins
  - Fuel Stations: petrol, gas, fill up
  - Supermarkets: groceries, shopping, food
  - Restaurants, Banks, Post Offices
  - Hardware, Electronics, Clothing Stores
  - And more categories!

• Location-Based Reminders
Save your favorite places and get notified when you're nearby. Perfect for errands and location-specific tasks.

• Time-Based Notifications
Get reminded at the perfect time. Set custom notification intervals from 1-60 minutes before your tasks are due.

• Guided Setup Experience
A beautiful onboarding flow guides you through:
  - Setting up your points of interest
  - Importing your existing calendar events and reminders
  - Finding and adding nearby stores, pharmacies, and fuel stations with one tap

• Beautiful, Intuitive Design
A clean, modern interface that makes managing tasks a pleasure. Mark tasks complete with a tap, swipe to delete, and stay focused on what matters.

• Privacy First
All your data stays on your device. Apple Intelligence processing happens entirely on-device - your tasks are never sent to external servers.

• Sign in with Apple
Secure, private authentication with your Apple ID. No passwords to remember.

Whether you're managing daily errands, work tasks, or personal goals, Smart Todo adapts to your lifestyle with intelligent, context-aware reminders.

Download Smart Todo today and experience AI-powered task management!
